# CHAMTABURU ECO VILLAGE
## Seven Cottage Identity & Guest Experience Plan

**Location:** Near Matha Forest, Baghmundi, Purulia, West Bengal  
**Concept:** Nature • Tribal Culture • Modern Comfort

---

# 1. Brand Concept

Chamtaburu Eco Village will offer seven individually named cottages rather than conventional room numbers.

Each cottage will have its own identity inspired by:

- Local trees and forests
- Purulia's natural landscape
- Hills and streams
- Tribal culture and village life
- The connection between people and nature

The cottages will maintain the same modern amenities and comfort, while each will have a small story and visual identity.

### Brand line

**Nature, Tribal Culture & Modern Comfort in Harmony.**

# 10. Room Nameplate Design

All seven cottages should use the same physical design so that the property looks professionally branded.

### Recommended material
**Wood + bamboo**

### Suggested size
**18" × 10"**

### Layout

```text
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│                              │
│        CHAMTABURU            │
│                              │
│       SHAL SHANTI            │
│                              │
│  Where the forest whispers   │
│          peace.              │
│                              │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

For each cottage, only the name and tagline change.

Use:
- Natural wood background
- Dark engraved/burnt lettering
- Small bamboo border
- Small leaf motif
- No excessive decoration

# 13. Central Garden

Create one common social space.

### Suggested setup
**Central lawn + fire pit + seating + warm lighting**

Use:
- Wooden benches
- Stone seating
- Low garden lights
- Lanterns
- Fire pit
- A few trees

Avoid too much plastic furniture in the main photographable areas.

---

# 14. Evening Lighting

Avoid relying on very bright white floodlights for photographs.

### Recommended atmosphere
**Warm 2700K–3000K lighting**

Use:
- Tree uplights
- Pathway lights
- Bamboo lanterns
- String lights
- Warm wall lights
- Low garden lights

The objective is: **bright enough for safety, soft enough for ambience.**

---

# 15. Bamboo Lantern Path

Along the main walkway, use bamboo or wooden lantern housings with warm LED lights.

This will be inexpensive but visually effective.

# 23. Overall Design Language

Keep the entire property within four visual principles:

### 🌿 Nature
Greenery, trees, plants, natural textures

### 🪵 Natural Materials
Wood, bamboo, stone, jute

### 🛖 Local Character
Subtle tribal and Purulia-inspired elements

### ✨ Modern Comfort
Clean rooms, good lighting, comfortable furniture and amenities

Avoid making the property look like a theme park.

The cultural elements should feel **authentic, tasteful and understated**.

# 25. Final Positioning

Chamtaburu Eco Village should not compete with conventional hotels by trying to look luxurious through expensive decoration.

Its strongest differentiator is:

**Modern cottages + Purulia nature + local culture + personalized cottage identities.**

The seven names should become part of the **guest experience**, not merely room numbers.

A guest should be able to say:

> "We stayed at Karam Kunja at Chamtaburu Eco Village."

rather than:

> "We stayed in Room 106."

That is what will make the seven cottages memorable and give Chamtaburu Eco Village its own identity.
